How the ratings work

Difficulty (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced): how much prior knowledge and effort it takes.

Time: estimated hours. Quick is 10 or fewer, Moderate is up to 30, Long is more than 30.

Value (1-5 stars): our estimate of how much it helps a resume, based on employer recognition and relevance to the major. It is an editorial judgment, not an official ranking, so use it as a guide.

Best value for effort sort: value divided by time and difficulty, so short, easy, high-value items rise to the top.

Points: hours times difficulty (certifications have a 10-point minimum). Quest share steps add 10. Progress saves in this browser only.
